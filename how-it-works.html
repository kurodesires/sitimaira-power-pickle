YOUR BOOKING GUIDE

From “let's play”
to game on.

Book pickleball or billiards online, or visit the venue to book in person. Staff update walk-in availability in the admin desk.

01

Choose what to play

Open the booking page and select the pickleball court or billiards table. Pickleball is ₱300 per hour; billiards is ₱100 per hour.

02

Pick a date and open hour

Choose a date on the calendar. Bookable hours are from 8:00 AM to 12:00 AM. Occupied slots are labeled so you can choose another time.

03

Enter your booking details

Provide your full name, contact number, and email so staff can coordinate your request.

04

Choose a payment method

Choose GCash or MariBank and follow the venue's payment instructions. Staff verify transfers manually while online checkout is being set up.

05

Booking confirmation

The admin reviews the booking request. A slot becomes occupied after payment is verified and the admin approves it. For walk-ins, staff mark the time unavailable; it reopens at the selected end time.
