YOUR BOOKING GUIDE

From “let's play”
to game on.

Everything you need to book pickleball or billiards at Sitimaira Power Pickle.

01

Choose what to play

Open the booking page and select the pickleball court or billiards table. Pickleball is ₱300 per hour; billiards is ₱100 per hour.

02

Pick a date and open hour

Choose a date on the calendar. Open hours are selectable. Occupied slots are labeled so you can choose another time.

03

Enter your booking details

Provide your full name, contact number, and email so staff can coordinate your request.

04

Choose GCash or MariBank

Choose the payment method you plan to use. Contact the venue for payment instructions. Keep the payment reference for your records. In this demo, staff must mark the transfer paid; a live payment gateway can do this automatically.

05

Pay to confirm your booking

Submitting the form creates an unpaid request only. The slot stays open until payment is received. This demo requires staff to mark a transfer paid; a live gateway can confirm payment automatically.
